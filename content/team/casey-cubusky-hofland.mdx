I want to produce games, publish stories, make videos and generally have a good time on Earth being a creative human. It is my wish to leave this world a little kinder than I found it, and generally inspire more acceptance towards minorities, neurodivergence and free thinkers.

Because all that is a little intensive however, I currently juggle building open-source game tools with being a full-time Web Developer building REST API's and Web Applications.

But plates are shifting, and I am carving out a company that will hopefully one day empowers game developers with the tools and consultancy they need to succeed. Check out [BuildingBlocks], [Stats], or whatever else we're working on this time.
